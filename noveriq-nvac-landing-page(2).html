WHY NVAC?

The foundation for your virtual assistant career

NVAC is a simple, beginner-friendly certification designed for people who want to become virtual assistants and prove the essential skills to get started.

The exam

A simple virtual interview to earn your NVAC.

After you pay for the exam, you'll be connected with an examiner. Once you agree on a suitable time, your assessment will take place through a virtual interview.

How it works

Personal, simple, and fully online.

There is no complicated testing platform. Your NVAC assessment is a scheduled virtual interview with an examiner who will evaluate your essential skills for starting as a virtual assistant.

What you should know

Essential skills for the assessment.

NVAC is designed as a foundation-level certification, so the focus is on practical basics rather than advanced technical expertise.

English Communication

Good conversational English and the ability to communicate clearly.

Computer Basics

General computer knowledge and confidence with everyday digital tasks.

Google Calendar

General knowledge of creating, managing, and organizing calendar events.

Microsoft Office

General familiarity with Microsoft tools such as Word and Excel.
